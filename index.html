Post-Production & Special Effects

Although many of my projects involve taking on multiple roles, through my short films and Final Year Project, I have become particularly apt at leveraging tools like After Effects and Blender to combine digital manipulation, motion graphics, and 3D modeling with real world imagery. This blend allows me to turn abstract or complex concepts such as psychological themes and surreal landscapes into immersive, high-impact visuals.

✦

Producing & Project Organisation

Bringing a project to life requires structured planning and adaptability under pressure. From concept development, budgeting, and location scouting to timeline tracking and team coordination, I manage every moving part of production to guarantee projects deliver on vision and deadline. Also, given my experiences with CIFF, and Priosma, this skill moves beyond the screen and translates to real world communication and organisation.

✦

Graphic Design

Through my time interning at Apple creating numerous logos, posters and icons for various projects in a fast paced environment, along with regularly creating marketing content for my own short films and projects, for example designing a book and posters for my Final Year Project, The New Age Enlightened Orthodox Brotherhood of the Righteous Divine, I construct strong visual systems that communicate narrative themes at a glance.

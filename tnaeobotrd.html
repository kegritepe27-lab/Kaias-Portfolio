The New Age Enlightened Orthodox Brotherhood of the Righteous Divine

IDI Graduate Award Winner 2026

A scalable framework for a transmedia Alternate Reality Game, and a fictional cult that blends belief, and absurdity. Guided by a structured Project Bible, it unfolds across TikTok, Instagram, and the web. It delivers immersive storytelling that critiques power and manipulation while remaining adaptable for future expansion.

Category: Transmedia ARG
Year: 2026
Timeline: 8 Months
Team Size: 1

Role:

Primary role was as a Transmedia Storyteller. This involved planning and setting up the Project Bible, narrative, design aesthetic and interactive elements across multiple platforms. My key responsibilities in the project included developing the core concept and narrative, structuring a scalable story across a one-month timeline, identifying platforms for content distribution, and exploring how audiences would engage with the project across these platforms. As the sole creator, I managed all stages of the transmedia design process, from initial concept development through to execution and implementation, including scripting, motion capture, animation, editing, and scheduling content for release.

Challenges:

Managing real-time community responses while maintaining strict adherence to the project narrative required an agile content pipeline. Balancing organic audience participation with structured lore milestones presented continuous developmental hurdles.

It was a very expansive project with a lot of moving parts, so a large focus was on making the project manageable and replicable for other solo creators to do their own versions of the project. This was one of the reasons for the Project Bible, which details all developmental steps necessary fot the project.

Results:

Across a 30-day multi-platform campaign on TikTok, Instagram, and web, this project validated a bespoke, motion-capture-driven transmedia pipeline capable of agile, audience-responsive storytelling. The ultimate output—a comprehensive 70-page Project Bible centered on the A.D.S (Artificial Divinity System)—establishes Version 1 not as a finished artifact, but as a canonically scalable ecosystem built for future narrative expansion.

In October 2026, the project won Best Experimental Design at the IDI Graduate Awards.

ESCA

ESCA is an application created to help people engage in positive forms of escapism, giving them a personalised selection of activities in their area. From trails, to events, to mental health resources, ESCA will benefit each user in a unique way.

Category: UX/UI
Year: 2024
Timeline: 3 Months
Team Size: 3
1 / 4

Role:

Although the 3 of us in the team worked in each stage of development, my primary role was as the UI/UX Designer for the project, drawing conclusions from our interviews, personas, and secondary research to design an interface that is both visually appealing, and has sufficient and relevant features for our target audience.

Why?:

Following an in depth research process into college students (aged 18-24) social media usage, we found that it is used as an escape so they don’t have to face the reality of their emotions. Due to this behaviour and activity their mental health and other areas of life are negatively affected thus causing torrential experiences in their lives and their own personal confidence and self-esteem. Our research group need a new and healthier outsource for their negative feelings and they need to be able to experience this aspect of life as it is natural.

Results:

After completing 6 seperate interviews, a questionnaire, drawing conclusions from research papers, and completing 3 user personas, we created an interactive prototype that was ready for development. This project gave us a deeper insight into the thinking of college aged students, helping with the development of future projects.
